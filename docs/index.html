What CodexBridge actually does

1. Receives intent

Requests can start as chat, issue, command, or internal workflow. The bridge gives them structure.

2. Coordinates execution

Agents and executors work through clear contracts, expected behavior, and controlled hand-offs.

3. Keeps evidence

Tests, contract checks, logs, diffs, and runtime behavior matter more than pretty claims.

Where the mobile app fits

CodexBridgeMobile gives quick visibility into what the bridge is doing: active tasks, notifications, health hints, and compact actions for when the operator is away from the main workstation.

Think of it as a portable control window — not the engine room itself.

Typical flow

  1. A request appears.
  2. CodexBridge interprets and dispatches it.
  3. Executors do the heavy work.
  4. CodexBridgeMobile mirrors progress and lets you react quickly.
  5. The human still validates the result.
